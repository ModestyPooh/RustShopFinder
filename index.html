  1. Open the map (G) and click a shop marker.
  2. Expand one or more shops so their items show, then take a screenshot (F12 in Steam, or Win+Shift+S).
  3. Expand the next shops and repeat until you've covered them all.
  4. Drop them all here, pick the map pin, check what it read, and save.
